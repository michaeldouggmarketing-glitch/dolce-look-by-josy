# Full magazine storefront

Mode: Experience with an explicit selection task.

User direction: turn the whole Dolce Look website into a premium shoppable magazine, comparable in usability to a printed retail catalogue, while retaining all real products, stock, favorites, sharing, service information and the existing website. Staff must switch between the current site and the complete magazine in Admin; the older product-only magazine remains available separately.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the entire storefront is a browsable publication, with cover, contents, category openings, real product leaves and service/back-cover chapters. The book is the interface; page turning must not turn into a sequence of product modals.
OWN-WORLD: straight authentic photos, quiet ivory paper, dark olive controls, oversized Italiana editorial type, restrained DM Sans utility type. Red belongs only to the paired original hearts. Pages settle aligned.
STORY: open the cover, navigate freely via contents or page gestures, choose real sizes/colors, save favorites without leaving the publication, review a selection and contact the team on WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: printed book cover with large genuine portrait left and brand/title/open action right; neutral work surface, thin real-stock strip, compact contents/selection controls and a persistent page navigator. Mobile stacks the photographic cover and copy while preserving sticky navigation.
FORM: code-led full-publication variant, chosen directly from the user's explicit brief. Signature interaction: bounded 820ms paper leaf with shaded reverse and live drag preview. No autoplay; keyboard, contents, search and explicit arrows remain equivalent alternatives.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Truth and recovery: only live available inventory supplies product pages; no synthetic prices, photos, stock or social feed. Actual last review comes from the existing server-stamped staff process. Selections persist locally and are validated against current stock before contact. Keep location by stable page ID/hash and session position. All page types must remain reachable from contents; all versions remain reversible.
