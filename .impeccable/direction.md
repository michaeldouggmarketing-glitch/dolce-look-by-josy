# Dolce in motion — full institutional site
## Strategy
Experience first, then selection and personal WhatsApp consultation. User explicitly rejected single-page LP, confirmed home/collection/product/brand/contact. Admin has one-unit stock constraints.
## Visual world
Ivory paper, deep wine-red, near-black; original nine fashion photographs, Italiana display and DM Sans controls. Composition changes materially from prior split hero. No fabricated history, proof metrics, reviews or prices.
## First viewport
One headline at left, three real portrait photographs in offset layers across center/right, primary Collection link grounded at bottom. Mobile presents title, layered portraits and action within viewport. The photos keep recognisable garments and anatomy.
## Signature interaction
Scroll holds the home composition and brings center portrait closer as side portraits peel away; headline recedes while next line joins. Wine-red ticker bridges to brand. A second pinned wardrobe reveals three different look scenes by upward masks while photo scale, typography and background change. This is an actual authored scene, not repeat fades.
## Motion grammar and constraints
GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis wheel duration .75, native touch. Short scrub .25-.3. Photo parallax and word-lighting statement, horizontal editorial scene, closing panel unframing, bounded ambient arc loops and button light sweep. Reduced motion makes panels flow vertically and keeps content available. Public stock claims conditional on live backend; sale must update every image and favorites. Code-first confirmed. Direction sampling endpoint unavailable (degraded); user brief is authority.
